About

I am a PhD candidate at Johannes Kepler University Linz as part of the Bilateral AI program, supervised by Martina Seidl and Johannes Fürnkranz. My research focuses on computational logic, particularly symmetries and symmetry breaking in QBF and DQBF.

Previously, I studied at the University of Amsterdam, where I obtained my BSc in Mathematics (2022) and MSc in Logic (2024).
